This is the site where I keep a record of things I learned while perusing the world, in case they may be useful to me or someone else later.

With the general decay, rot, and enshittification of the world and the Internet, I like having a somewhat reliable place
to reference for those bits and bobs of information that I keep needing again and again.

Think of me as a bit of a hoarder, raven, loot goblin, and general lover of esoteric snippets of arcane and mundane knowledge. 
My ADHD makes me very prone to forgetting things, and just generally scatter-brained as all hell. Simultaneously, I am 
extremely impatient to look for things that I could've sworn I put somewhere around here.

Way back in the day this site used to live on my personal computer as a bunch of folders grouped roughly by topic
and labeled "Things", e.g. I'd have a folder titled "HTML Things", and each file would be a plain text file that roughly described 
what was in it e.g. "Loading an external stylesheet.txt."

As the world evolved and I was dragged kicking and screaming into more modern ways of 
doing things, this structure evolved into a bunch of folders on dropbox, then Google Drive, then Obsidian, and the file format eventually graduated 
to Markdown.

The basic idea remained the same however, until finally I discovered you could have a free website on GitHub, and that GitHub did you 
the courtesy of reading your markdown files and rendering them into decent-looking, though simple, web pages.
By this point I had already discovered how to sling 1s and 0s at a computer and have it do something with them, so I started thinking about creating my own static site generator.

Then I was dragged by my hair a few more years into the future, and discovered there were already a plethora of those available. So here I am, slowly converting my ±30 years' old pile of folders and text files
into a bunch of static pages on GitHub. Until I get distracted by something newer and shinier I guess.
